About Career Hub

Career Hub is designed as a student-focused platform for discovering education and career resources in one place.


Students can explore colleges, exams, mock tests, career guidance, internships, articles, communities and professional resources.


The platform is intended to help students organize their career research and discover useful official resources.

Our Mission

  • Make career information easier to explore
  • Help students discover opportunities
  • Encourage practical skill development
  • Support internship preparation
  • Promote continuous learning

🔒 Privacy Policy

Career Hub is a demonstration/student-resource website. This single-page version does not create a real user database or store login passwords on a server.


Do not enter sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information or other confidential information into demonstration forms.


External websites linked from Career Hub have their own privacy policies and terms. Please review the policies of those websites before providing personal information.
